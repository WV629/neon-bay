Neon Bay is a complete open-world crime game in the spirit of GTA 6, built as plain static files with zero network requests. Explore a fictional sun-soaked city on foot and by car, steal cars, dodge traffic and pedestrians, raise your wanted level and outrun the Neon Bay PD, and work through a five-mission story for fixer Mara Voss — from your first stolen wheels at the Docks to the Big Score at Bayfront Bank. It has a live HUD with a minimap, a day/dusk/night cycle, synthesized WebAudio sound and music, combat on foot and from the driver's seat, save/load, and a start screen, pause menu and game-over flow. Everything — the city, the characters, the story, the art and the sounds — is original.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome. WASD to move/drive, E to enter and exit cars, mouse aim and click to shoot, F to punch, R to reload, Space for the handbrake, Shift to sprint, P to pause, S to save, M to mute.
